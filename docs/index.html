Looks and camera LUTs, both handled

Creative LUTs expect Rec.709. Camera conversion LUTs expect raw log. LUTviewer knows the difference, guesses from the file name, and lets you switch with one click.

Works with H.264, HEVC (8 and 10-bit) and ProRes clips, plus JPG, PNG, TIFF and HEIC stills.
